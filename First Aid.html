First-Aid

During this basic First-Aid course, you learn the important basic First-aid skills,including basic life support and the use of an AED. You learn how to bring a person to safety, how to access their condition and alert emergency services. In this course you learn how to administer First-Aid to someone who is unresponsive, bleeding,chocking or suffocating,has a burn,cut,graze,bruise,sprain,bone fracture or poisoning.

The requirements of the course is only grade12, reason being so is so that we could accommodate the less fortunate. Everyone deserves a chance at following their dream regardless of whatever situation they are in. this course is offered at the cost of R1500.
