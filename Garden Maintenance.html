Garden Maintenance

This course covers crucial aspects of domestic gardening, including watering, pruning, and planting. Participants will learn to manage their gardens efficiently, with insights on optimizing water usage, addressing the needs of indigenous and exotic plants, mastering trimming techniques, and plant propagation methods. Whether you're a seasoned gardener or a beginner, this course provides valuable knowledge for all enthusiasts. Join us for informative sessions covering a wide range of garden maintenance topics:

  • Efficient Watering: Learn to water your garden effectively for optimal plant growth and water conservation, considering the unique needs of different plant varieties.
  • Masterful Pruning: Perfect the art of pruning to ensure the health and shape of your plants, guided by our experts across various plant types.
  • Plant Propagation: Explore plant propagation techniques, from seeds to cuttings, to multiply your favorite plants and enhance your garden.
  • Diverse Planting: Enhance your garden with a variety of plants using guidance on planting techniques for different types, creating a vibrant and thriving space.

Join our weekly Garden Maintenance sessions, every Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday, from 11:30 am to 1:00 pm. Our expert instructors provide practical tips and hands-on demonstrations, empowering you with the skills to create and maintain a stunning garden. Enroll now to start your journey to gardening success!

Instructor Profiles

Thabiso Modise

Thabiso specialises in Watering Techniques and with over 12 years of experience as a horticulturist, Thabiso is passionate about efficient watering practices. He has worked on various conservation projects and is known for his expertise in promoting healthy plant growth while conserving water resources.

David Smalls

David has been a professional gardener for more than two decades. His skill in the art of pruning has transformed countless gardens. He will teach you the secrets of maintaining the shape and health of your plants through proper pruning.

Emma Hampton

Emma is a botanist and has extensive knowledge of plant propagation. She has conducted research on plant genetics and propagation techniques. Join her to explore the fascinating world of multiplying your favorite plants. She also has a diverse background in landscaping and has created stunning gardens with a wide variety of plant species. She will guide you through planting techniques for different types of plants to create a vibrant and thriving garden space.
