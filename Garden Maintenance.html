Garden Maintenance

This course's purpose is to provide essential information on watering, pruning, and planting in a domestic garden (IIE, 2023). Participants will gain valuable insights into managing their garden, including optimizing water usage, understanding the watering needs of both indigenous and exotic plants, effective trimming techniques, and methods for propagating plants. Whether you're a seasoned gardener or just starting out, this course offers valuable knowledge for all enthusiasts. Join us for informative sessions covering a wide range of garden maintenance topics:

  • Watering Techniques: Learn how to efficiently water your garden to promote healthy plant growth while conserving water resources. Discover the unique watering requirements of different plant varieties.
  • Pruning Strategies: Master the art of pruning to maintain the shape and health of your plants. Our experts will guide you through the pruning process for various plant types.
  • Propagation Methods: Explore the fascinating world of plant propagation. From seeds to cuttings, you'll gain insights into multiplying your favorite plants and expanding your garden.
  • Planting Diversity: Diversify your garden with an array of plants. We'll provide guidance on planting techniques for different types of plants, helping you create a vibrant and thriving garden space.
Sessions are held every week, starting at 11:30 am and concluding at 1:00 pm on Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Our experienced instructors will walk you through each topic, offering practical tips and hands-on demonstrations. Whether you have a green thumb or are just getting started with gardening, our Garden Maintenance course will empower you with the knowledge and skills needed to create and maintain a beautiful and flourishing garden. Enroll today and embark on your journey to becoming a confident and successful gardener!
