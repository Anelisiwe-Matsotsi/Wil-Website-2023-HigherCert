Garden Maintenance

This course's purpose is to provide essential information of watering, pruning and planting in a domestic garden (IIE, 2023). People will be given necessary information about plants and how to manage water usage limitations and understanding the watering needs of indigenous and exotic plants, trimming and the process of multiplying plants, as well as methods for planting various kinds of plants. Every session will start at 11:30am till 13:00pm on Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday.

Price: R750
