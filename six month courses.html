About: EMPOWERING THE NATION

Empowering the nation is a business that was established in 2018 in hopes to empower domeatic workers as well as gardners all over Johannesburg with skills that can help them improve their wages.People are trained on both six-month long Learnerships and six-week long Short Skills Training.
