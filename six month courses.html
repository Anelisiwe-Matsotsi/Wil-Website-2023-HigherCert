SIX MONTHS COURSES

The six-month programmes take place over 12 weeks. The following courses are provide:First Aid, Sewing, Landscaping, and Life Skills.


First Aid

Learn how to bring a person to safety, how to access their condition and alert emergency services. In this course you learn how to administer First-Aid to someone who is unresponsive, bleeding,choking or suffocating,has a burn,cut,graze,bruise,sprain,bone fracture or poisoning.

Sewing

You will learn the basics of fabric(cutting) and working with sewing patterns. It will also focus more on additional sewing techniques patterns. The courses includes personal use of a sewing machine, mannequin and a working space.We also offer a library of designs with their graded patterns

Landscaping

We offer in-depth and informative training courses for all fields throughout the landscaping industry through a merged classroom based and practical experience, tailoring training to suit your learning desires. We guarantee our courses will always overreach expectations.

Life Skills

Navigating the the different aspects of everyday life requires plenty of practical skills. Problem solving, cooking skills, basic comunication, general health maintenance and similar abilities enable individuals to function effectively.
