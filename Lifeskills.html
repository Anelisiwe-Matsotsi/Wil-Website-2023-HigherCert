Life Skills

Navigating the the different aspects of everyday life requires plenty of practical skills. Problem solving, cooking skills, basic comunication, general health maintenance and similar abilities enable individuals to function effectively and meet the demands of modern life. Important traits like independence, problem-solving ability, flexibility, and personal development can be fostered with the use of life skills. Developing these abilities might also make you stand out anywhere. There are many obstacles in life, like budgeting, relocating to a new location, or enrolling in college. The abilities you need to overcome those obstacles will change based on your age, stage of life, and career objectives. No matter where you are in life, you can keep on top of your personal development with Empowering The Nation.

Interested in acquiring new life skills to meet personal demands and increase employability? Empowering the Nation offers just that for you. This course covers a lot of topics and ou can enroll and get a degree in a relevant topic. The duration of this course is six months at the cost of a once of payment of R1500. However, should you need a payment arrangement you will visit the financial office.

Instructor Profiles

Kathryn Druskoff

Kathryn Druskoff holds a Bachelor's degree from Wellesley College, a Master's degree in Education from Simmons College, and advanced studies in Social Work, Counseling and Special Education. Kathryn is experienced working therapeutically with children, adolescents and adults teaching coping skills, social skills, self-regulation and executive functioning skills. She has been trained in Adaptive Behavioral Therapy for remapping of neurological pathways. She is a certified Special Education teacher in Empowering The Nation with more than 15 years of experience.
