Landscaping

We believe the world's biggest issue is hunger. It literally impacts everything. Until now, the knowledge and training of nutrient-dense diet has seemed out of reach for many. But with Empowering The Nation, anyone can grow health. The Empowering the Nation is an Instituiton that trains and empowers people. Empowering the Nation offers landscaping, the upliftment of landscapers and entry-level garderners. At Empowering The Nation our aim is to nourish, develop and provide stimulating education in all aspects of landscaping no matter the experience of you nor your company. We offer in-depth and informative training courses for all fields throughout the landscaping industry through a merged classroom based and practical experience, tailoring training to suit your learning desires. We guarantee our courses will always overreach expectations by offering you with the latest recommendations and training, and going beyond and above the industry guidelines.

The requirements of this course is only grade 12.Empowering the Nation seeks unskilled people and garden enthusiasts. The duration of this course is 6 months at the cost of a once of payment of R1500. However, should you need a payment arrangement you will visit the financial office.

Instructor Profiles

Jacob de Klerk

My grandparents were great plant people. My grandfather bred Camillias under high-canopied pine trees in Breaux Bridge, Louisiana. Walking under those pines was transcendental for me. As I grew older, the pine forest experience would happen under many other trees, in may other places. I would build little house-like structures and fountains and pretend I was somewhere else. Wanting to continue experiencing that transcendental pine forest feeling in new places, off to landscape design school I went.

Michael Adams

Michael is a landscape horticulturist with extensive experience in plant identification, culture, and use. He is an expert on the selection of landscape plants and strongly supports the notion that there are no bad plants - only good plants planted in the wrong place. As an outdoors enthusiast, Michael has a special interest in native plants and plant communities and his experiences have given him a unique, big-picture perspective when it comes to plant growth requirements and the use of plants in designed landscapes.

Akhona Johnson

Akhona holds a B.A. in plant ecology from the University of Colorado and an M.S. in plant physiology from the University of MN. She has served as a board member of the Society of Wetland Scientists and the MN Native Plant Society, instructed biology and ecology, and spoken to many professional and interest groups since 1980. As Trustee of the Markhart Organics Endowment Fund at the UMN, Akhona enjoys interacting with undergraduates who enthusiastically aspire to better the world through ecologically based farming systems.
