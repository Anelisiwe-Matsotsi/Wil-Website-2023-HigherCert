Sewing

This course includes professional guidance from fashion designers and it will focus on deepening and refreshing your skils on the sewing machine. You will learn the basics of fabric(cutting) and working with sewing patterns. It will also focus more on additional sewing techniques patterns. The courses includes personal use of a sewing machine, mannequin and a working space. We also offer a library of designs with their graded patterns for use outside and in class free of charge. However, some equipments are for rental.

The requirements of this course would only be grade9,since you would only need the skill and not much of an academic background. The Duration of this course is six months and after that you get your certificate for completing the programme. The total course fees for the Sewing is R1500.
